01

The Thesis

On August 2, 2026, the EU AI Act's general-purpose and high-risk provisions become enforceable across every member state. Non-compliance fines reach €35 million or 7% of global revenue, whichever is greater — a regime stricter than GDPR.

A $5.6 – 7.4 billion AI-compliance market is forming around this deadline, but today's RegTech and MLOps platforms were built for cloud models and centralised logs. None provide tamper-evident, device-local audit trails for AI that runs at the edge — precisely where the Act's transparency, logging, and incident-reporting obligations land hardest.

Minima is the only edge-native, quantum-resistant Layer-1 engineered to run a complete validating node on any device, producing cryptographic compliance proofs where the inference actually happens. The asymmetric bet: a ~$3.7M market cap in front of a multi-billion-dollar structural market that only one architecture can serve.
