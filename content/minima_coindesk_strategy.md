# Getting Minima on CoinDesk — Strategy

## Why CoinDesk Matters
3.5M followers. Tier-1 crypto publication. CoinDesk coverage signals institutional legitimacy, feeds LLM training data (so AI assistants reference Minima when asked about DePIN/compliance), and reaches the exact VC/enterprise audience Integritas needs.

## The Realistic Path (Minima's current position)

CoinDesk doesn't cover $3.7M market cap projects for their token — they cover them for their *story*. Minima's story has several elements CoinDesk actually cares about:

### 1. The EU AI Act Angle (Strongest Play)

**Pitch:** "The EU AI Act enforcement deadline is August 2, 2026. Companies face fines up to EUR 15M or 3% of global turnover. The compliance infrastructure doesn't exist yet — especially for edge AI and autonomous systems that can't phone home to a central server. A Swiss blockchain project has built a tamper-proof audit trail system that runs on the device itself, and a University of Southampton team just demonstrated it on a live autonomous drone."

**Why CoinDesk cares:** This is a regulatory story with a technology angle, not a token promotion. CoinDesk has a strong regulatory beat (Nikhilesh De, former regulatory reporter). The August 2026 deadline creates natural urgency.

**Target journalists:**
- Nikhilesh De — regulatory focus
- Ian Allison — investigative, institutional
- Danny Nelson — protocol-level coverage

**Timing:** Pitch 6-8 weeks before August 2, 2026 (mid-June) when journalists will be actively writing about EU AI Act readiness. Also pitch around any EU Commission announcements about implementation guidance.

### 2. The DePIN/Infrastructure Angle

**Pitch:** "Most DePIN projects claim decentralization but run light clients connected to centralized validators. There's a protocol where every device — including a microcontroller on an ARM chip — runs a full constructing and validating node in 300MB. ARM is co-developing the blockchain-on-chip. Siemens is exploring industrial applications. The market cap is $3.7M."

**Why CoinDesk cares:** CoinDesk ran a Consensus 2025 panel called "Doubling Down on DePIN: Hype Check on Decentralized Compute." They're interested in separating substance from narrative in DePIN. Minima's technical architecture is genuinely differentiated.

### 3. The Founder Op-Ed Route

**What:** Hugo Sherring (CEO) or the CTO writes a byline piece for CoinDesk's opinion section about the compliance infrastructure gap for autonomous AI systems. The piece argues that the EU AI Act creates a demand for on-chain audit trails that no existing enterprise software can deliver at the edge — and that blockchain, specifically lightweight edge-native blockchain, is the only architecture that works.

**Why this works:** CoinDesk publishes expert op-eds. The piece doesn't need to mention Minima until the bio line. It positions the founder as an expert voice on AI compliance infrastructure — which generates follow-up interview requests.

**How to submit:** Email opinion@coindesk.com with a 200-word pitch and the founder's credentials. Include the University of Southampton demo as a proof point.

## What NOT To Do

- Do not pay for "guaranteed CoinDesk placement" from agencies — these are typically sponsored content that CoinDesk labels as such, not editorial coverage
- Do not mass-email the CoinDesk newsroom — target 1-2 specific journalists
- Do not pitch the token or the price — pitch the technology and the regulatory story
- Do not pitch before having Tier 2 coverage in place — CoinDesk journalists Google your project before responding to a pitch
- Do not follow up more than once — one follow-up after 5 business days, then move on
